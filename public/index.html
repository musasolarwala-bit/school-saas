School users enter their school slug. Super-admins log in with email + password only.

What Stage 1 includes

  • Multi-school tenants with fully isolated data (tested at API and database level)
  • Per-user accounts: school_admin, teacher, parent, student — each with their own login
  • Classes and students with role-based visibility (teachers see only their classes; parents see only their child)
  • Super-admin dashboard: list schools, suspend / activate a school
  • Phase 1: 12 staff roles with a capability matrix, duration packages (12 / 24 / 36 months, Lifetime) with a 30-day free trial, and per-school branding on the login page

Stage 2 will add attendance, fees, results, shop, uniforms, exam papers, accounts and notifications — ported from the offline School Manager app.
